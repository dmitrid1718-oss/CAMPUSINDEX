Index definition: average private-sector hourly earnings ÷ CPI for tuition, other school fees and childcare, divided by the same ratio in March 2006, × 100. The CPI category includes childcare and other school fees; it is not college tuition alone, a published college budget, a dorm price or individual household purchasing power. Source: BLS wages via FRED and BLS tuition/school-fees/childcare CPI via FRED. Historical readings change only when verified data change.
